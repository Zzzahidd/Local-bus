# লোকাল বাস (Local Bus)

> "একটা বাস, একটা পথ, আর কিছু পুরোনো গান।"

**লোকাল বাস (Local Bus)** is an immersive, atmospheric nostalgia web experience. It is inspired by the feeling of sitting in the back seat of an old South Asian local bus at dusk, traveling along familiar roads while listening to classic songs.

---

## ✨ Concept & Experience

- **Cinematic Journey**: A continuous, full-screen video background capturing the motion and ambiance of a local bus route.
- **Local Time**: Displays the visitor's local time based on their timezone.
- **Shared Presence**: A real-time active user indicator showing how many people are experiencing the journey together right now.
- **Curated Nostalgic Music**: An integrated music player playing a continuous, nostalgic YouTube playlist with rotating vinyl album artwork.
